How it works

1
Install the extension

One click from the Chrome Web Store. No account required. No data collected from you.

2
Browse any financial page

VETT detects tickers on Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Reddit, X/Twitter, and dozens of other sites automatically.

3
Hover over any ticker

The card appears with the score (0–100), the current market regime, and the top signals driving it.

4
Scores refresh daily

The engine re-runs every day and VETT pulls the latest brief — and every card shows the date its score is from.
