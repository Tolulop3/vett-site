Methodology

How VETT scores a stock

Every score you see in VETT is a number between 0 and 100, produced by InvestOS — the engine VETT runs on. This page explains what goes into that number, how often it changes, and what it deliberately doesn't claim.

The score

A score of 0–100 maps to one of three verdicts. VETT never tells you to buy or sell — it tells you whether a stock is worth a closer look.

75–100
Investigate

Worth a closer look right now.

50–74
Watch

Not a clear signal either way.

0–49
Pass

The data doesn't support attention right now.

What goes into it

InvestOS combines technical, fundamental, and market-context signals into a set of pillars, weighted dynamically by the current market regime rather than fixed once and left alone:

MomentumTrend strength, moving averages, relative strength rating
GrowthRevenue and earnings growth
ValueP/E, PEG, and fair-value checks
Dividend / IncomeYield, payout history, upcoming ex-dividend dates
SafetyDebt/equity, ROE, balance-sheet quality
Volume / LiquidityVolume accumulation vs. distribution

On top of those pillars, InvestOS also factors in analyst consensus, insider buying/selling clusters, and macro/news signals (e.g. a sector headwind or tailwind from a detected event) — these show up as the specific bullet points behind the "Why?" panel on a card.

Market regime

Every score is shown alongside the current market regime (e.g. Bull, Neutral, Defensive, Cautious). The same score can mean something different depending on regime — VETT surfaces that tension directly on the card rather than leaving you to infer it, when the two are in conflict (a strong score in a defensive regime, or a weak score in a bull market).

Untracked tickers

Not every North American ticker is in InvestOS's daily-scored universe. If you hover one that isn't, VETT shows a card built from live technical data instead — price, trend, momentum, and 52-week range — with a clearly-labeled estimated score. This is explicitly not an InvestOS score, and the card says so.

Refresh cadence

InvestOS re-runs daily. VETT caches the latest run and refreshes that cache roughly every hour, so a card is never more than about an hour behind the day's latest run — and every card shows the date the score is from, so you can always tell exactly how current it is.

Coverage

VETT covers North American markets only — US-listed and TSX. Tickers listed elsewhere are flagged as out of coverage rather than shown a guess.
