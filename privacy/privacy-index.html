Privacy Policy — VETT

Last updated: October 24, 2026

VETT — Stock Score Hover is a Chrome extension that shows a daily quantitative score for stock tickers on web pages you visit. This policy explains what data VETT does and does not handle.

The short version

VETT collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, and no tracking. We do not sell or share any data — there is none to sell.

What VETT does not collect

VETT does not collect, store, or transmit: your identity or contact details; your browsing history; the content of pages you visit (ticker detection happens locally, in your browser); your financial information; or your location.

What is sent over the network

To display scores, VETT sends only stock ticker symbols (for example, "NVDA") to the InvestOS score service that powers it, over HTTPS. No identifier tied to you is attached to these requests. VETT also downloads the daily score brief from the same service.

What stays on your device

Your watchlist and VETT's local cache of scores are stored in your browser's extension storage, on your device only. Removing the extension deletes them.

Third parties

VETT contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the support link on VETT's Chrome Web Store listing.
