§ 01

The Three Verdicts

ScoreVerdictWhat it means
75–100■ InvestigateWorth a closer look right now. The data supports your attention.
50–74■ WatchNo clear signal either way. On the list, not on the desk.
0–49■ PassThe data doesn't support attention right now. Keep walking.

VETT never says buy or sell. It says whether a stock has earned your next hour of research — the decision stays where it belongs.

§ 02

What Goes Into the Number

Momentumtrend strength, moving averages, relative strength
Growthrevenue & earnings growth
ValueP/E, PEG, fair-value checks
Dividend / Incomeyield, payout history, ex-dates
Safetydebt, ROE, balance-sheet quality
Volume / Liquidityaccumulation vs. distribution

Six pillars, weighted by the market regime of the day rather than fixed once and forgotten — plus analyst consensus, insider clusters, and news signals, which appear as the grounds on each slip. The full method is published on the methodology page; the engine's pick-by-pick record is logged, not curated.

§ 03

How It Works

01Install

One click from the Chrome Web Store when it opens. No account, no sign-up, nothing collected.

02Read as usual

Yahoo Finance, Google Finance, Reddit, X, the news — VETT finds the tickers on the page by itself.

03Hover

The verdict slip appears: the score, the regime, and the grounds behind it. Three seconds, no new tab.

04Tomorrow, again

The engine re-runs daily and every slip carries the date its score is from. Stale numbers say so.
